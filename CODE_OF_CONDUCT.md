# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, practical place to discuss macOS audio recording software. Participation is expected to be welcoming and professional, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or opinion.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific when discussing bugs, designs, and reviews.
- Assume good intent and make room for different levels of technical experience.
- Keep feedback focused on the work rather than the person.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish real recordings, private device data, credentials, or personal contact information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, and disruptive conduct are not acceptable. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issue threads.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, remove, or close content that violates these expectations, and may limit participation when necessary to protect the community or users' privacy.
